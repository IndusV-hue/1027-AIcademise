Our philosophy

Sustainable use, not shortcut use.

AI can make you a sharper student or a lazier one — the difference is entirely in how it's used. These are the rules we build every feature around.

01 / Explain first

Understanding before answers

Every response walks through the reasoning first. The answer is the last thing you see, not the first.

02 / Practice, not proxies

You do the working out

The assistant sets up problems and checks your steps — it doesn't write the assignment for you.

03 / Honest limits

Knows when to say "ask your teacher"

For grading, judgment calls, and anything high-stakes, we point you back to a real person.

How it works

Four steps, every session.

01

Bring your topic or your confusion

Paste a problem set, a paragraph you don't understand, or just say what you're stuck on.

02

Get the reasoning, not just the result

Every explanation is broken into the same steps a good tutor would use — no skipped logic.

03

Practice with questions that adapt

Follow-up questions get harder or easier depending on what you actually got wrong.

04

Track what's actually sticking

Your ledger shows which concepts are solid and which need another pass before the test.
